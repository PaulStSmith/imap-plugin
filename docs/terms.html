IMAP Mailboxes Beta is currently a private beta Codex-exclusive plugin. These terms describe the intended use while the plugin is being developed and tested. It is not currently offered as a general ChatGPT GPT, hosted mailbox service, or non-Codex assistant integration.

Use of the plugin

You are responsible for the mailbox accounts you connect and for complying with your email provider's terms. Prefer app passwords when your provider supports them.

Read-only v1

The free v1 surface is designed for read access: folder listing, search, message reading, summaries, and attachment metadata.

Paid mail actions

Paid mail actions, including SMTP send and mailbox state changes, require an active license or subscription and should use clear review before messages are sent.
