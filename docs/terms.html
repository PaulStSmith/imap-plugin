Terms of Service

IMAP Mailboxes Beta is a hosted MCP plugin for Codex and compatible GPTs. These terms describe the intended use while the plugin is being developed and tested. Claude, Gemini, and other non-OpenAI assistant systems are outside the project scope; any use there is at your own risk.

Use of the plugin

You are responsible for the mailbox accounts you connect and for complying with your email provider's terms. Prefer app passwords when your provider supports them.

Read-only v1

The free v1 surface is designed for read access: folder listing, search, message reading, summaries, and attachment metadata.

Mail actions

Mail actions, including SMTP send and mailbox state changes, require an entitled IMAP Mailboxes installation and should use clear review before messages are sent.
